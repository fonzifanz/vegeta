### *Written on February 23, 2026*

Hello everyone, I hope you are all doing well. Here is an update on what is going on in my life and what I have been up to. 

For the first 2 weeks of March (next week & the week after), I will not be around much with you and my other people. I know this is sudden, but I have a good reason this time. 

The reason is that my sister is having her kid around that time (the second week). I have been told I need to go with my mom to visit her here and there, supporting her at the biggest moment in life. 

I am thinking of making a mini documentary as I want to put my film-making skills to effective use. It is more of a personal project, but my sister could use it for something like a memory to keep and store. I have always been into photography & film, but never found something to do with it. So, I am going to use it for that. 

Another thing is to do well in school, as I am in the last classes before I complete my undergraduate degree. I am aiming to graduate within 2-3 years from now, as next semester will be my second year. 

As for myself, I am seeing part of my family changing. At the beginning of this year, my sister moved out of the house to move in with her fiancé. I finally have my own room after all this time stationed in the living room. Recently, my brother got everything settled for his wedding in May. Now he just must wait until the time comes. He is also planning to move out after the wedding. 

I hope I get to experience all this. It is something I cannot miss and really want to be there for the proudest moments in life. This is a learning experience for me as I grow and finally see what true love means. As for myself, never being in a relationship, I learned a lot about it from both my siblings and friends. 

I just have not found my person, which was always hard for me. And it still is. I once thought I would die alone or something. I do not know, but that is not the case anymore. The interesting thing is, my siblings have found their loved ones as high school sweethearts. Me? I am about to be in my second year of college, and I am still single. 

But hey, it is not bad being that way. There's still time to do so. It is simply hard for me, as I have what defines me. Not that I am looking for it right now, but hopefully, I will not be alone as much anymore in the future. I just want someone who can accept my autism and what makes me unique. I hope so. 

I know I said a lot here, but it is what I have been having in my mind to speak about with everyone. And this is what I have to say about this. If you made it this far, thank you. I appreciate it. 

Have a good day.
