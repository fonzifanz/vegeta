<h3> I am leaving Roblox </h3>

### *Written on August 5, 2026*

Roblox is currently on the brink of crashing.

I took my leave recently, after 10 years on the platform. I have been deciding on it ever since they age restricted the platform. I mean, ever since moderation saw no improvement at all. 

Now that I am saying this, I will tell you this. I almost got groomed by someone there when I was fifteen. (2021) I will not say why because I do not want to think about it again, and it was weird.

Besides that. When COVID hit all of us, I opened up to this game every day. At the time I was in a new school, away from friends I remember hanging out with a few months before that. Then I felt lost. I started talking to people through Roblox around my age, in communities that were a safe place to be in. From it, I met wonderful and great people who were there for me. It helped me get over my problems and through the struggle of being isolated because of COVID.

As much as I want to say I still talk to them today, I unfortunately do not. They all moved on as we got older and life separated us away by going onto what we want to do. I never had a best friend, but I met my first ever one through Roblox. 

I know, I know, you should not trust people through a screen. But what was my other option if I did not know where to reach out with my old friends? I did not have their contact or anything because of my introverted self. Roblox was a place where I can talk to someone, even if it is a stranger I can get to talk about something.

That was 5-3 years ago. I am now in a completely different environment where I do not have to rely on that anymore. As much as I cherish it, it is where I started off to meet people like you. I have a small IRL friend circle that I see once to twice a month. I love them, as friends. But having you all as well makes me happy that I am not suffering anymore. I am happy where I am now with my social circle and cannot ask for anything better.

As I was remembering all the things I did and writing this, I felt nostalgic. Since I was playing for a decade now, I remember how enjoyable the platform was back then. I was so into tycoons and simulators; I would play the second I can get on to play after school. Those were the good old days. 

I have to say something about Roblox in the future. I want to say that you had a good run. You will be missed, sure. But under your current leadership, the state of moderation, the lack of genuinely good games, you are losing players at a rate you are not worrying about. You are worried about investors. Roblox was not about investors when it started. It was about the players and the communities on the platform. That is what made Roblox great.

In its current state, I cannot figure out why people still play, when you are locked out behind an age check to gain access. Why do people complain about moderation not caring about the people and instead replace hard working people with Artificial Intelligence? I know most of the player base is under eighteen, but still, you should be able to at least chat.

The company's reason for the current performance (their stock, down by 70%) is "lack of new viral games." There is much more than that I already went over earlier, but I guess Roblox does not want to admit it to their investors. When schlep and others got banned for helping the people on Roblox, it absolutely gave me confirmation that Roblox is not safe for anyone. It is not just predators; it is more than that. I recently found out about other groups that are on Roblox right now, preying on innocents to do the most disgusting stuff a human can imagine. And if Roblox does not do anything about it, I am not going to support them.

This is the end of Roblox, for me.
