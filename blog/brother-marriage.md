<h3> My brother got married! </h3>

### *Written on May 23, 2026*

Small blog post, but yeah, my brother got married to his highschool sweetheart! I am so proud of how far he has gotten in life. Unfortunally he moved out, but that means I finally have my own room for the first time since i was born. Yeah, that long ago, I didn't have my own room. Until now. It feels nice and kind of a relief that I now have my own space I can be comfortable in. He moved not far from here, and good enough I get to see him once or twice a week.

Congrats to my brother for a major milestone, and may his life come with new ambitions.
