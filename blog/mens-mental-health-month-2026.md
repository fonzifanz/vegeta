<h3> June is Men's Mental Health Awareness Month. </h3>

### *Written on June 4, 2026*

I want to come on here to say Happy Men’s Mental Health Awareness Month.
Many men struggle with depression, anxiety, and other mental health challenges, yet too few reach out for help. Facing what you’re feeling can be difficult, but avoiding it often makes the burden heavier.

Whether it’s therapy, talking to someone you trust, or simply taking small steps to care for yourself, progress starts with action. Mental health is like any other part of life: It takes effort, but that effort can build confidence, resilience, and a better future.

Never be ashamed of struggling. What you’re feeling is human, and it does not define you. With time, support, and courage, things can change.

I hope you all have a good day
